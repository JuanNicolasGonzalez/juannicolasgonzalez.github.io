Profile

I am an engineer and researcher focusing on transportation systems, energy, and data-driven policy analysis. My professional background spans private and public sector roles, as well as research centers and think tanks, allowing me to combine technical analysis with policy-oriented research. My work examines transport policy, emerging mobility services, and their implications for sustainability, efficiency, and energy transitions.
